ℹ️ About OmniMod

OmniMod is a powerful, multi-tenant SaaS platform that lets you create and manage moderation bots for both WhatsApp and Telegram groups from a single dashboard. Whether you run a study group, a community, or a business channel, OmniMod automates the repetitive work — deleting links, filtering abuse, greeting new members, running quizzes, and opening/closing your groups on schedule.

Built by educators and community managers, OmniMod was created to give group admins their time back. No coding, no servers, no technical headaches — just scan a QR code or paste a bot token and your moderation bot is live.

📋 Rules & Regulations

By using OmniMod, you agree to the following rules:

  • No abuse: Bots must not be used to harass, spam, or harm any individual or group.
  • Lawful use only: You are responsible for ensuring your bot's actions comply with all applicable laws and platform terms (WhatsApp/Telegram).
  • Respect platform limits: Do not attempt to bypass WhatsApp or Telegram rate limits or automation restrictions.
  • Account security: You are responsible for keeping your login credentials and bot tokens secure.
  • No resale of access: Your subscription is for your own use; do not resell or share access to the platform.
  • Fair use: Excessive or abusive usage may result in rate limiting or account suspension.
  • Founder discretion: The OmniMod team reserves the right to suspend accounts that violate these rules.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how we handle your data:

  • Account data: We store your email, name, and password (hashed) to authenticate you. We never share your credentials.
  • Bot credentials: WhatsApp auth sessions and Telegram bot tokens are stored securely and used only to operate your bots. We do not read or sell your group messages.
  • Activity logs: We retain moderation logs (e.g. deleted messages, actions taken) so you can monitor your bots. These are visible only to you.
  • Payments: Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your full card details.
  • Data deletion: You can delete your bots and account at any time; associated data is removed.
  • Third parties: We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.
