ℹ️ About OmniMod

OmniMod is a powerful, multi-tenant SaaS platform that lets you create and manage moderation bots for both WhatsApp and Telegram groups from a single dashboard. Whether you run a study group, a community, or a business channel, OmniMod automates the repetitive work — deleting links, filtering abuse, greeting new members, running quizzes, and opening/closing your groups on schedule.

Built by educators and community managers, OmniMod was created to give group admins their time back. No coding, no servers, no technical headaches — just scan a QR code or paste a bot token and your moderation bot is live.

🔒 Privacy Policy

  • Account data: We store your email, name, and password (hashed) to authenticate you. We never share your credentials.
  • Bot credentials: WhatsApp auth sessions and Telegram bot tokens are stored securely and used only to operate your bots.
  • Activity logs: We retain moderation logs so you can monitor your bots. These are visible only to you.
  • Payments: Payment processing is handled securely. We do not store your full card details.
  • Data deletion: You can delete your bots and account at any time; associated data is removed.
  • Third parties: We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.
